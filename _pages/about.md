What I'm working on
======
Besides exam studying and professional work, there are numerous things I am currently working on. First, is enhancing my technical expertise in various softwares like Excel and Python through self-teaching methods. Second, fine-tuning my Spanish which I am ashamed to say still not reached proficiency, after which, I plan to jump into German. Third, growing an online presence is extremely important, especially as digitization and technology grows. This will be in the form of my YouTube channel and Substack. Fourthly and at last, I have a great interest in studying deep mathematics, as regrettably I did not formally study this in college. It only occurred to me in my final year of school, that everything on a practical basis can fundamentally be reduced down to math. Whether it be to compute bond prices, learn to code, or even build a house, most applications require math. Therefore, learning math in a deep level makes sense.
